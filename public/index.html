How to use:
  1. Receive a file: Scan the QR code under "Receive File" with your phone camera, then choose and upload from your phone. Click "Download File" when it appears to save to this computer.
  2. Send a file: Drag & drop any file or aim and take the shot into the basketball hoop! Scan the generated QR code on your phone to download it.
  3. Auto-delete: Files are automatically deleted from the server immediately after download or after 5 minutes.
