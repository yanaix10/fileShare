fileShare

Quick file sharing between your computer and phone

How to use:
  1. Receive a file: Scan the QR code under "Receive File" with your phone camera, then select and upload the file from your phone. Click the "Download File" button when it appears to save it to this computer.
  2. Send a file: Select a file under "Send File" and click "Generate Send QR". Scan that QR code with your phone to download it.
  3. Auto-delete: Files are automatically deleted from the server immediately after download or after 5 minutes.
